How to choose

How to choose a tree service company in Beavercreek — the same checks apply whether you found a listing here or a truck on the street.

  1. 1. License

    Ask for the license or registration the company uses to work in Ohio. Write down the number. TreeList does not invent license IDs on this page.

  2. 2. Local jobs

    Ask for recent addresses in Beavercreek or the surrounding towns — not a generic photo set. Local access (hills, alleys, overhead lines) changes the job.

  3. 3. Written scope

    Get the work in writing: what comes down, what stays, who hauls wood, whether the stump is included, and how they protect the roof, fence, and lawn.

  4. 4. Reviews with addresses

    Prefer reviews that mention a street or neighborhood in Beavercreek. Star averages with no job location are easy to fake. TreeList does not publish star ratings or review counts.

  5. 5. Who shows up

    Ask who is on site: employees or subcontractors, how many people, and who is the decision-maker if the plan changes mid-job.

  6. 6. Warranty

    Ask what is warranted (cleanup, turf, a missed hanger) and for how long. “We stand behind our work” is not a warranty.

  7. 7. Emergency vs planned

    If this is not an emergency, say so. Storm work and planned tree service are different queues. Do not let a salesperson treat a Saturday trim like a rescue.
