What is on a city page

  1. A locked H1: Best {Service} in {City} — 2026
  2. A disclosure that this is a directory and paid spots are labeled
  3. How to choose: license, local jobs, written scope, reviews with addresses, who shows up, warranty, emergency vs planned
  4. A national cost range cited to Angi — not a local survey
  5. Five FAQs that match the on-page questions in schema
  6. Listings from a data file, or an empty state with the quote form
